Not just a safari

Meet the many sides of Jawai.

The first image many travellers associate with Jawai is a leopard among granite rocks. But the region is more than a wildlife encounter. Its character is shaped by rocky terrain, Jawai Bandh, birdlife, pastoral traditions and the everyday relationship between people and the land.
