Map — Jawai Bandh region

The marker shows the Jawai Bandh area in southern Rajasthan — leopard-hill country between Udaipur and Jodhpur. Safari lodges and villages sit in the granite hills around the reservoir.

Getting to Jawai

By road

Common bases: Sumerpur and Sheoganj on the Pali–Sirohi corridor. Most lodges arrange pickups from Udaipur (~3–4 hrs) or Jodhpur (~3 hrs).

Nearest rail / air

Falna and Udaipur are useful rail stops; Maharana Pratap Airport (Udaipur) and Jodhpur Airport are the usual flight hubs for Jawai trips.

On the ground

Safaris run at dawn and dusk in open jeeps. Carry sun protection, layers for cold mornings (Oct–Feb), and respect village privacy when near Rabari settlements.
